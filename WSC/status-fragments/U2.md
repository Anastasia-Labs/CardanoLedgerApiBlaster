## 2. FINDING — `LeafSet.p4`'s "`LR5`'s rewarding clause" remark is too quick

`LeafSet.p4`'s docstring says the `DelegateSeize` arm's conclusion
(`seizeScopedToNodeOf`, a `Rewarding seizeCred` entry in the REDEEMER MAP) is
upgraded to "`seizeCred ∈ txInfoWdrl`" by "`WSC.LR5`'s rewarding clause
(`validScriptInfo`)".

That does not follow. `validScriptInfo`
(`CardanoLedgerApi/V3/Contexts.lean:979-1002`) constrains the purpose of the
script *currently running* — here the issuance minting policy — whereas the
`Rewarding seizeCred` entry belongs to a different script. The fact is still a
real ledger rule (Conway UTXOW builds `scriptsNeeded` FROM the withdrawal map, so
a redeemer entry for a rewarding purpose cannot exist unless that reward account
is withdrawn from), but it is a statement about the redeemer map as a whole and is
NOT entailed by `LR5` as written.

Isolated as `Composition.SeizeWdrlOfScoped`, with the discharge named: strengthen
`WSC.LR5` (or add `LR_REDEEMER_PURPOSES_REAL`) with an audit row citing
`Conway/TxInfo.hs` `transTxRedeemers` + the UTXOW `scriptsNeeded` rule. Not done
here — it is an axiom-base change whose audit row does not exist yet.

## 3. FINDING — `LR_BALANCE_SLOT` is NOT a "mechanical" CLAB lemma

§9.5 called the derivation "mechanical but unwritten". Two facts found while
writing it:

1. **`valueOf` is NOT additive over `merge`.**
   `Composition.merge_not_additive_without_canonicity` is a `native_decide`
   counterexample: `a = [("b",{t:1}), ("a",{t:1})]` (canonical entries, wrong
   order), `b = [("a",{t:1})]`. `merge`'s `cs_visit`
   (`CardanoLedgerApi/V1/Value.lean:151-164`) is a SORTED merge — its own comment
   says "assume `Value` is well-formed" — so it emits `"a"` twice, and `valueOf`
   returns at the FIRST key (`:95-103`), reporting 1 instead of 2.
   Consequence: the derivation MUST carry a sortedness predicate (`CanonV`,
   §3.5) and `merge` must be shown to preserve it, because `valueSpent` folds
   `merge` over every input.
2. **CLAB supplies nothing towards it.** There is no lemma anywhere in
   `CardanoLedgerApi/` relating `valueOf` to `merge`, `withoutLovelace`,
   `valueSpent` or `valueProduced`. The analogous development exists one layer
   down for PCB's CIP-153 `ValueRep` — `PlutusCore/Value/Algebra.lean`
   `unionInner_lookup` (:561-660), `unionOuter_lookup` (:809-910),
   `unionInner_sortedFrom` (:493-561), `unionOuter_sortedFrom` (:746-809), ~330
   lines — and is the TEMPLATE, but is not reusable: `merge`/`valueOf` on
   `List (Data × Data)` and `unionOuter`/`lookupCoin` on `ValueRep` are different
   functions and bridging them is a proof of the same size.

What IS proved here: `valueOf_withoutLovelace` (unconditional for
`cs ≠ adaSymbol` — this is where the FEE drops out), `sumInsIf_split` /
`sumOutsIf_split` (base + off-base partition the inputs/outputs),
`isBalanced_nonAda_eq` (`LawfulBEq Value` turns `isBalanced`'s `==` into a real
`Value` equality), and the assembly `LR_BALANCE_SLOT_of_valueAlgebra`.

Residue, fully enumerated: `ValueAlgebra` (3 fields: `ofValueSpent`,
`ofValueProduced`, `ofMergeMint`) and `LedgerCanon` (canonicity of a real
transaction's values, i.e. `CanonV` read off `validTxOutValue`/`validMintValue` —
mechanical unfolding of two nested `let rec`s, no algebra). Instantiating both
deletes the axiom; the replacement is one line.
